24/7 Frozen Pipe Thawing

Frozen Pipe Repair in Nampa, ID

A frozen pipe is a burst pipe that has not happened yet. When a faucet gives only a trickle on a cold Nampa, Idaho morning, ice has blocked the line somewhere upstream, and the pressure building behind it is what splits the pipe. Our emergency frozen pipe service thaws the line safely and checks it for damage before water flows freely again.

Frozen pipe repair covers three jobs: finding the frozen section, thawing it without harming the pipe, and fixing whatever let it freeze in the first place.

Where Pipes Freeze

The Spots That Freeze First in Nampa Homes

Pipes freeze where they meet outside air, not in the middle of a heated room. These are the usual suspects.

Right Now

What to Do When You Suspect a Frozen Pipe

  • 1Leave the faucet open. A slightly open tap lets water move as the ice melts and relieves pressure.
  • 2Warm the area, not the pipe with flame. Use a space heater at a safe distance, a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use a torch or open flame on a pipe or near framing.
  • 3Know where the main shutoff is. If the pipe has already split, water will spray when it thaws. Be ready to close the main.
  • 4Check other fixtures. If only one faucet is affected, the freeze is on that branch. If nothing runs, the freeze is near the main or service line.
  • 5Call if you cannot reach it. Pipes inside walls or deep in a crawlspace are safer left to a plumber with proper thawing equipment.
Thawing and Repair

How a Plumber Thaws and Checks the Line

We locate the frozen section by working back from the dead fixture and checking where the pipe is exposed. Thawing uses controlled heat such as heat guns, heat mats or electric pipe-thawing equipment, applied from the faucet end back toward the ice so meltwater has somewhere to go.

When water flows again, we check the thawed section and the fittings on either side for splits. Copper can crack along its length and PEX fittings can pull loose, and some damage only shows once pressure returns. Any split is repaired the same visit. If the pipe has already let go, our split pipe repair crew takes over.

Prevention

Stopping Next Winter's Freeze

The city lists 19.1 inches of average annual snowfall, and cold snaps in the teens arrive most winters. The fixes are straightforward and usually cheap compared with a burst line, and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) already requires water piping in exterior walls or unheated spaces to be protected from freezing.

Insulate every supply line in the crawlspace and garage, and close foundation vents for the winter if your home's design allows it. Replace old hose bibs with frost-free sillcocks, which shut the water off inside the heated wall. Our faucet and sink repair team handles hose bib swaps.

For lines that cannot be moved or insulated well enough, a listed heat cable installed to the manufacturer's instructions keeps them above freezing. Before leaving for a winter trip, keep the heat on and consider shutting off the main.
