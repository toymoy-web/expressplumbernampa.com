Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Nampa Express Plumbing collects when you visit this website or call us, and how it is used. Last updated September 2026.

FormsNone on this site
CallsMay be recorded
Selling dataWe do not

Information We Collect

This website has no contact forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

When you call, the phone system records the number you called from, the time and duration of the call, and the general location associated with your number. Calls may be recorded for quality, training and routing purposes.

If you give your name, address or details about your plumbing problem during a call, that information is used to arrange service.

How Information Is Used and Shared

Call information is used to connect you with a licensed plumbing contractor serving your area, to schedule and deliver service, and to measure which calls came from this website.

Your information is shared with the contractor handling your job and with service providers that operate our phone and hosting systems. We do not sell personal information.

Your Choices and Contact

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@expressplumbernampa.com. We may keep records where required by law.

This site may link to other websites, such as a map provider. Their privacy practices are their own. Questions about this policy can be sent to the email above.
