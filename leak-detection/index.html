Hidden Leak Detection

Leak Detection in Nampa, ID

Leak detection is the work of finding exactly where water is escaping before anyone opens a wall or digs a trench. In Nampa, Idaho, the calls usually start with a water bill that jumped, a meter that keeps turning with everything off, a warm spot on the floor or a damp patch under the house.

We isolate the system in sections, then use acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and pressure testing to narrow the leak to a small area. You get a clear answer about where the leak is and what fixing it involves.

Sound Familiar?

A High Water Bill, a Running Meter, or Water You Cannot Trace

Most people who need leak detection do not know where the water is going, only that something is wrong. The meter keeps turning after every tap is closed. The bill doubled with no change in habits. Water appears at the edge of a floor or under a sink with no obvious drip.

In Nampa there is an extra wrinkle. Many homes have two separate water systems: city drinking water through the meter, and canal-fed pressurized irrigation on its own line. A soggy lawn in summer with a still meter points to the irrigation side. A spinning meter points to the drinking-water side. Knowing which system is leaking saves hours.

A quick self-check: close every fixture, note the meter reading, wait an hour without using water and read it again. Any change means water is leaving the system somewhere.

Method

How Isolation and Instruments Narrow It Down

We start at the meter and work inward. Closing the main shutoff at the house tells us whether the leak is outside, on the service line, or inside. Closing individual fixture and branch valves narrows it further.

Once the zone is known, instruments do the rest. Acoustic equipment picks up the hiss of pressurized water escaping underground or in a slab. Thermal imaging shows the temperature signature of a hot-water leak behind drywall. A pressure test on an isolated section confirms whether that section is holding, the same kind of test the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) requires on new supply piping before it is covered.

If the leak sits on the line between the meter and the house, the repair is covered on our service line page. Leaks at fixtures are often simpler than they look.

Rural Properties and Private Wells

Homes on private wells have no city meter to watch, so leaks show up differently. A well pump that cycles on and off with no water in use is the classic sign, and it wears out the pump and pressure switch quickly.

The same isolation approach works. We close valves in stages and watch the pressure tank gauge instead of a meter. Acreage south of Nampa, including around Melba, often has long buried lines to shops and barns where these leaks hide.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost of Finding a Leak

Detection is priced separately from the repair, because finding the leak is its own job. Knowing the exact spot usually lowers the repair cost, since less gets opened up.

Insurance often covers damage from a sudden hidden leak and sometimes the cost to access it. It rarely covers the pipe repair itself. Keep the detection report for your claim.
