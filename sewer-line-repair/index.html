Sewer Line Repair in Nampa, ID

Sewer line repair fixes the pipe that carries everything from your house to the city sewer, usually after roots, a crack, a sag or a separated joint has turned a routine clog into a recurring one. In Nampa, Idaho, we answer sewer backups 24/7 and start every repair the same way: a camera in the line so you see the damage before paying to fix it.

Many sewer problems can be fixed at one spot without replacing the whole line. The camera tells us whether that is true for your house.

What to Do When Sewage Backs Up

  • 1Stop all water use. No flushing, laundry, dishwasher or showers. Every gallon has to go somewhere, and on a blocked sewer it comes up the lowest drain.
  • 2Keep people and pets away. Sewage carries bacteria. Keep children and pets out of the affected room.
  • 3Do not run chemical openers. They will not clear a root mass or a broken pipe and make the line hazardous to open.
  • 4Locate the cleanout. It is usually a capped pipe outside near the foundation. Tell the dispatcher where it is.
  • 5Ask a neighbor. If homes on both sides are backing up, the public main may be the problem, and the city needs to know.

Repair Options

Spot Repair, Lining or Replacement

A spot repair digs down to one damaged section and replaces just that piece. It is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound, and it is common when a single joint has let roots in.

Trenchless lining slides a resin-soaked liner into the old pipe and cures it in place, creating a new pipe inside the old one without a long trench across the yard. It works when the old pipe still holds its shape. It does not fix a sag, because the liner follows whatever grade the old pipe has.

Excavation for private sewer lines outside the building is regulated in Nampa, and the city requires qualified excavation contractors for that work. Whichever method fits, the job is permitted and inspected.

Spot repair: one damaged section, rest of line sound.
Lining: pipe still holds shape, avoids a long trench.
Neither fixes a sag. A belly needs excavation and regrading.
Prevention

Keeping a Repaired Line Clear

Roots will come back to any gap that remains, so a repaired joint or a lined section is the lasting fix. Cutting roots alone buys months, not years.

Grease, wipes and paper towels cause the rest. A line with a history of clogs benefits from periodic high-pressure jetting, which cleans the pipe wall rather than just opening a hole. If you are unsure whether a line needs repair at all, a standalone video inspection of the sewer answers that before you commit.

Pricing

What a Sewer Repair Costs Depends On

Depth, length and what sits on top of the line drive most of the cost. A shallow spot repair under a lawn is a very different job from one under a driveway.

Standard homeowner policies usually exclude sewer line repair as wear and tear. Some carriers sell a service line endorsement that covers it, so check your policy.
