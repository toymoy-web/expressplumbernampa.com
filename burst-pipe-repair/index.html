First Five Minutes

What to Do When a Pipe Bursts

  • 1Close the main shutoff. Usually where the supply line enters the house: a crawlspace access, the garage wall or a utility room. If you cannot find or turn it, tell the dispatcher.
  • 2Turn off the water heater. Set a gas unit to off or pilot, or switch off an electric heater's breaker, so it does not run with the supply shut.
  • 3Kill power to wet areas. Only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Otherwise stay out and wait.
  • 4Open a faucet at the lowest point. This drains remaining water out of the lines and away from the break.
  • 5Photograph everything. Take pictures of the break, the water and any damaged belongings before cleanup, for your insurer.

Nampa Winters and Why January Is the Busy Month

The City of Nampa lists an average January low of 21.1°F, and the Boise airport weather station about 16 miles away recorded minus 25°F on December 22, 1990. It does not take a record cold snap to split a pipe. A few nights in the teens is enough for a supply line in a drafty crawlspace or an exterior wall.

The practical point: the pipes that burst are almost always the ones exposed to outside air. Insulating crawlspace lines, closing foundation vents in winter and using frost-free hose bibs removes most of the risk before it becomes a 2 a.m. call.

The Repair

How We Repair a Burst Pipe

Once the water is off, the plumber opens just enough of the wall, ceiling or floor to reach the break and assess the pipe on either side. On sound copper or PEX, the damaged section is cut out and replaced with new pipe and fittings.

If the surrounding pipe is corroded galvanized or brittle, a short patch will not hold for long, and we will say so. After the repair, the line is pressure-tested before the wall is closed, and we check the rest of the exposed run for other weak spots.

Water that has already soaked into drywall, insulation or subfloor needs proper drying. That is a separate service, handled on our page for drying and restoring water damage with its own dedicated line.
