Nampa

Every Nampa ZIP Code

Inside Nampa we cover all three standard ZIP codes. ZIP 83651 takes in downtown and the north and west sides, where the housing ranges from early railroad-era streets to large 2000s subdivisions. ZIP 83687 runs east toward Meridian, and 83686 covers the south side out toward Lake Lowell.

Emergency plumbing, drain and sewer service, water heater work and leak detection are available in all three. Homes inside city limits get plumbing permits from the City of Nampa Building Department. For the full list of services, see plumbing services.

Nearby Towns

Neighboring Communities We Serve

Each town page covers that community's water, housing and most common plumbing calls.

Caldwell

Canyon County's seat, just west of Nampa, mixes older neighborhoods near its restored Indian Creek downtown with rural properties on private wells. See plumbing in Caldwell for ZIPs 83605 and 83607.

Kuna

South of Meridian in Ada County, Kuna grew from a farm town into a large subdivision community over the past 25 years. Details for ZIP 83634 are on the Kuna plumbing page.

Middleton

The oldest settlement in Canyon County sits beside the Boise River, and its population has climbed fast in recent decades. Visit Middleton plumbing for ZIP 83644.

Star

Straddling the Ada and Canyon county line, Star went from about 1,800 residents in 2000 to over 11,000 in 2020. Local details for ZIP 83669 are on the Star plumbing page.

Melba

A small farming town about 12 miles south of Nampa near the Snake River, surrounded by acreage on private wells. See Melba plumbing for ZIP 83641.

Getting to You

How Distance Affects Response

Nampa sits in the middle of the service area, with Caldwell and Middleton to the west and north, Star and Kuna to the northeast and southeast, and Melba to the south. Most of the area is reachable by the I-84 corridor and state highways that tie these towns together.

When you call, the dispatcher gives you an honest timeframe based on where you are and what is already scheduled. We do not promise a fixed arrival window we cannot keep, especially for rural addresses farther out.

Emergencies such as a burst pipe or sewer backup are prioritized over planned work across the whole area.

Permits Depend on Where You Are

Plumbing permits follow jurisdiction lines, not ZIP codes. Inside Nampa city limits the city issues them. Other cities issue their own or rely on the state, and unincorporated county areas generally go through Idaho DOPL.

The plumber confirms which office applies to your address and pulls the permit as part of the job, so you do not have to sort it out yourself.
