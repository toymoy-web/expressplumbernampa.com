North Nampa and Lakeview Park

Plumber in North Nampa, Nampa, ID

North Nampa is the part of the city on the far side of the Union Pacific tracks from downtown. The City of Nampa's 2008 North Nampa Revitalization Strategy studied 1,107 acres there, bounded by the railroad, the interstate and a boulevard.

It is home to Lakeview Park, one of the city's oldest parks, along with older residential streets such as Park Lane and the corridors of Indian Creek and Mason Creek.

Where It Is

North Nampa on the Map

The 2008 strategy describes North Nampa as bounded by rail, interstate and boulevard. It notes that the historic rail crossings at 9th Avenue North and 14th Avenue North were closed in the early 1980s, leaving three crossings into downtown along 1.4 miles of shared frontage.

The plan names several neighborhoods inside the area, including Lakeview, Park Lane, Gateway, Indian Creek and Mason Creek. It describes historic homes along Park Lane that look out over Lakeview and Maple Grove Parks.

Parks and Water

Lakeview Park, Lake Ethel and the Creeks

Lakeview Park covers more than 44 acres. Local reporting traces the land to an 1888 homestead and notes that Lake Ethel, built to store irrigation water from Mason Creek, once sat here. Maple Grove Park now occupies the former Lake Ethel site, according to the city's plan.

Indian Creek and Mason Creek both run through the study area. For homeowners, the creeks and irrigation history are a reason to keep an eye on crawlspace moisture, while remembering that damp ground is not proof of a plumbing leak. A meter test tells the two apart.

Owners and Landlords

Safety Steps for Older North Nampa Plumbing

The city's plan noted that many homes here are rentals needing maintenance or repair. A few habits keep small problems from growing.

  • 1Know who owns which line. The City of Nampa says homeowners are responsible for repairs to water and sewer lines on their private property.
  • 2Camera the sewer before buying. A short video of the lateral tells a buyer what condition the line is in before it becomes theirs.
  • 3Check under the house each season. A look in the crawlspace after the first hard freeze and again in spring catches leaks early.
  • 4Fix drips promptly. A dripping faucet or running toilet in a rental adds up on the bill and is usually a small part to replace.
Cost and Safety

Sewer Lateral Repairs and Safety in North Nampa

A sewer lateral repair is priced by what the camera shows: how deep the pipe is, how far it runs to the main, whether tree roots are involved and whether one section or the whole line is damaged. That is why a camera inspection comes before any quote for digging or lining.

If water is standing in a crawlspace, do not go in while any wiring, outlets or a furnace could be wet. Shut off the main water and the power to the area first, then call. For homes that water lawns from the house supply, the UPC requires backflow protection on any sprinkler system connected to drinking water.
