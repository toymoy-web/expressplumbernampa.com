Toilets

Toilet Repair and Installation in Nampa, ID

Toilet repair in Nampa, Idaho covers the handful of problems every household runs into sooner or later: a toilet that runs on its own, one that rocks or leaks at the floor, a clog that the plunger will not clear, or a cracked tank. We fix the part that failed, and we install new toilets when replacing makes more sense.

Most toilet repairs are quick and inexpensive when caught early. The expensive ones start as a slow leak at the base that quietly rots the subfloor for months.

Hard Water

Why Nampa Fill Valves Wear Out

Parts of Nampa get well water at the harder end of the city's reported range of none to about 7 grains per gallon. Minerals build up on fill valve seals and flush valve seats, which is why a toilet can start running or refilling slowly after only a few years.

A running toilet is not just noise. Water escaping past a worn flapper goes straight to the sewer, and it shows up on the bill. If your meter creeps while every tap is off, check the toilets before booking a hidden leak search. A few drops of food coloring in the tank that appear in the bowl within 15 minutes confirm a leaking flapper.

Dye test: color in the bowl means a leaking flapper.
Meter creeping with taps off: check toilets first.
Mineral crust on the fill valve: replace, do not scrub.

Before You Call

What to Do When a Toilet Is Leaking or Overflowing

  • 1Close the supply valve. It is the small oval handle on the wall or floor behind the toilet. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  • 2Do not flush again. A second flush on a clogged toilet sends the bowl over the rim.
  • 3Mop and dry the floor. Standing water at the base soaks into the subfloor. Dry it and note whether it keeps coming back.
  • 4Check the ceiling below. If the toilet is upstairs, look for a stain or drip on the ceiling underneath.
  • 5Take a photo. Useful for a landlord, or if the leak has damaged flooring.
Replacement

When a New Toilet Makes More Sense

Replace rather than repair when the tank or bowl is cracked, when the toilet clogs constantly because of an old low-performing design, or when repairs are adding up. Porcelain does not fail often, but a hairline crack in a tank can let go without warning.

Replacing a toilet in the same location is generally treated as a fixture replacement, which the City of Nampa exempts from its plumbing permit requirement. Moving a toilet to a new spot is different, because it relocates drain piping and needs a permit. If the floor flange is broken or corroded, we replace it during the install so the new toilet sits solid.

New toilets sold today meet federal limits of 1.6 gallons per flush, and many use 1.28 gallons or less. For a bathroom remodel that also moves the tub or shower, our shower and bathtub installation team can coordinate the drain work.

Pricing

What Toilet Repair and Replacement Cost Depends On

Most toilet repairs are one part and a short visit. Replacement cost depends mostly on the toilet you choose and the condition of the flange underneath.

Insurance rarely covers the toilet itself, but sudden water damage from a failed supply line may be covered.
