When You Call

What to Have Ready Before You Call

A few details help the dispatcher send the right person with the right equipment.

  • 1Your address and ZIP code. It confirms coverage and helps route the call.
  • 2What the water is doing. Leaking, backing up, not flowing, or not heating, and where in the house.
  • 3Whether you found the shutoff. If water is spreading, close the main first.
  • 4Appliance details if relevant. Water heater fuel type, tankless error codes or the faucet brand.
  • 5Who approves the work. Owner, tenant or property manager, so pricing goes to the right person.
