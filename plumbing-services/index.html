By Category

Find the Service You Need

Each card groups related services. Every link leads to a full page with local details, costs and common questions.

Water Heaters

Tank past ten years or leaking at the base? Start with water heater installation and replacement.

No hot water on a tank that still has years left usually means water heater repair.

Error codes or cold bursts point to tankless water heater service, including descaling.

Drains and Sewers

Drain cleaning and unclogging clears slow or stopped drains, 24/7.

Lines that clog again after snaking need hydro jetting.

See inside the line before paying for repairs with a camera inspection of the pipe.

Roots, cracks or sags at one spot call for sewer line repair, while damage along the whole run means sewer line replacement.

Leaks and Pipes

A running meter or a jump in the water bill starts with tracking down the hidden leak.

Warm spots on a concrete floor point to slab leak detection and repair.

Water spraying from a split line needs burst pipe repair, and a trickling faucet in January needs frozen pipe repair.

Whole-System Pipe Work

Rusty water and weak pressure in an older home often mean it is time for repiping.

The buried pipe from the meter to the house is covered by water line repair and replacement.

Sprinkler and building cross-connections are protected with backflow testing and prevention.

Water Quality

Scale on faucets and short water heater life can be solved with water softener installation and repair.

Water filtration installation, including reverse osmosis, targets taste, odor and specific contaminants.

Fixtures and Bathrooms

Toilet repair and installation fixes running, rocking or leaking toilets.

Drips, cabinet leaks and split hose bibs fall under faucet and sink repair.

A jammed or leaking disposal needs garbage disposal repair, and bathroom updates use shower and bathtub installation.

Gas and Commercial

New appliance lines and leak repairs are covered by gas line installation and repair.

Restaurants, shops, offices and rentals use our commercial plumbing service.

Water Damage (Separate Line)

Soaked floors and walls after a leak need water mitigation first, then water damage restoration.

These two services use their own 24/7 line, shown on their pages.

How We Work

The Same Standards on Every Job

Every job starts with a diagnosis, and you approve the price before work begins. Estimates on planned work, such as a water heater replacement or a repipe, are free.

Permitted work is permitted. Inside Nampa city limits that means the City of Nampa Building Department, and outside the city it usually means Idaho DOPL. Work is done to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) that Idaho's plumbing code is based on.
